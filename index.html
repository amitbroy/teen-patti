🃏 Hand Rankings (High → Low)

  • 🔥
    Trail (Three of a Kind)
    Three same-rank cards. A-A-A is highest.
  • 💜
    Pure Sequence
    Three consecutive cards, same suit.
  • 🔵
    Sequence (Straight)
    Three consecutive cards, mixed suits.
  • ♠
    Color (Flush)
    Three cards, same suit, not consecutive.
  • 👥
    Pair
    Two cards of same rank. Higher pair wins.
  • 🎴
    High Card
    None of the above. Highest card wins.

🕹️ How to Play

  • Everyone pays the Boot to enter the pot
  • Chaal — match the current bet to stay in the round
  • See — peek at your cards (doubles your future bets)
  • Fold — give up this round and lose your bet
  • Show — challenge another seen player to reveal cards; best hand wins the pot
  • Last player standing wins the pot automatically
  • A-2-3 counts as a valid Sequence (wrap-around)
